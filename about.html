About us

Who we are

A lounge for grown, stylish nights out

Ocean Lounge DC is where vibrant energy meets sophisticated style. We built it for people who want great cocktails, great music and a crowd that came to enjoy themselves.

Step in after a long week, celebrate a milestone or host your whole crew. With weekly events, three levels and an ambiance that is both chic and welcoming, your weekend starts here.

Join the family

Don’t just become a member. Become family.

Joining unlocks exclusive perks and a community that thrives on connection, joy and relaxation.

  • Stars on bookings, tickets and RSVPs
