New Year’s Eve 2026 at Ocean Lounge DC

Dec 31Thursday
9 PMDoors open
21+Valid photo ID

Say goodbye to 2026 and welcome 2027 in true style. An evening of sophistication, excitement and incredible vibes at 1220 H St NE, Washington, DC 20002.

What to expect

  • Champagne toasts to start the new year
  • Three separate DJs playing three different genres
  • An upscale, vibey atmosphere on every level
  • Signature cocktails and premium hookah
  • The best crowd and energy in the heart of DC

Ticket and event policy

  • All guests must be 21+ with a valid photo ID
  • Dress to impress: upscale attire, no athletic wear or sneakers
  • Tickets are non-refundable and non-transferable
  • Arrive early to avoid long lines
