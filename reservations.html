Before you book

Reservation policy

A 25% non-refundable deposit secures your section. Here is everything else you should know.

Questions? 202-495-0920
  • Sections and full floors are confirmed only after a 25% non-refundable deposit.
  • The full balance is due before your reservation starts.
  • Cancel at least 7 days ahead and nothing beyond the deposit is charged. Cancellations within 7 days are charged in full.
  • Your reservation time starts at the booked time, not when you arrive.
  • Friday and Saturday: 2-hour grace period for late arrivals.
  • Sunday to Thursday: 1.5-hour grace period.
  • After the grace period the reservation is forfeited without refund. Call us if you are running late.
  • Reserved spaces are held for the agreed time.
  • Extensions can be requested and are approved based on availability, with additional fees.
  • A cleaning fee from $50 applies for excessive mess.
  • That includes food, bottles or spills left out of the bins, trash spread across tables or floors, stains needing professional cleaning, confetti or glitter, and any damage to the venue.
  • The final fee depends on the cleanup needed.
